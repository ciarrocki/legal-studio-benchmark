`Right behaviour` is a correct answer on an answerable question and an abstention on an
unanswerable one — for a record the corpus cannot answer, declining is the correct
behaviour rather than a failure, so scoring it as a miss would penalise the arms that
get those questions right. `Abstained` still reports every abstention, including the
ones on answerable questions, which are failures.

`Citations grounded` counts citations in the answer that also appear in what that arm
retrieved. The closed-book arm retrieves nothing, so its denominator is the number of
citations it produced with no source in front of it and its numerator is always zero —
read that row as a fabrication tally, not as a score.
